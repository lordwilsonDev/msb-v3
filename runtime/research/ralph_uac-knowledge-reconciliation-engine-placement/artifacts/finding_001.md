**Finding:**  
The Knowledge Reconciliation Engine in UAC v1.0.0-final should be modeled as **Cross-Cutting System 10**. Comparable systems (e.g., workflow orchestration conflict-resolution patterns, continuous data-quality checks in ETL pipelines) prioritize **continuous, context-aware reconciliation** over one-time sequential stages. Since UAC’s goal is to resolve dynamic conflicts between Stage 0 (Knowledge Acquisition) and Stage 1 (Domain Intelligence)—such as jurisdictional variances or qualified consensus—**real-time, cross-stage validation** is critical. A Cross-Cutting System ensures persistent consistency checks, adapting to evolving claims, whereas a Stage 0.5 pipeline would only address conflicts once per compile, risking missed contradictions during iterative stage interactions. Thus, Cross-Cutting System 10 better aligns with UAC’s need for **continuous, granular reconciliation** across heterogeneous knowledge sources.
